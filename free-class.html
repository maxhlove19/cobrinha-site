Kids & teens · ages 3–16

Book your child's free trial.

Pick their age group, pick a day, and it's booked. Trials are free and one-on-one with an instructor, on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. Gear rental is $15, refunded in full if they sign up.

What happens next

Their first visit.

  1. You're booked. We confirm by text or email.
  2. Sign the waiver. Online after you book (here, it creates their profile), or at the desk.
  3. Arrive 15 minutes early. Meet the coach and rent a gi ($15, refunded when they sign up).
  4. Train. A warm-up, a few techniques one-on-one, and a look at their class.
  5. Decide later. Nobody sells you at the desk.
