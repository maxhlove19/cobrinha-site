Your coach

Ursula Valverde, black belt.

Ursula has taught for more than twenty years and leads every women's class here. Her classes start from what actually happens, a grab, a shove, being pinned, and build the technique that gets you out and away, then the sport on top of it.

  • Women-only room, women's changing room and showers
  • Self-defense first: wrist grabs, hair grabs, being pinned, getting up and getting away
  • All levels together; beginners partner with patient training partners
  • Train the women's class only, or add it to the full schedule. Many do both.

Why women train here

What you get.

Real self-defense

Technique built so a smaller person controls a bigger one. You learn to stay calm, get up and get away, and you practice it until it is automatic.

Strength and endurance

An hour on the mat is strength, cardio and mobility at once, with people cheering you on. Fitness comes from the training, not before it.

A room that has your back

Women who started where you are, a coach who remembers your name, and a wider academy with a 16-year record of looking after beginners.

Questions

Before your first class.

Is it really women only?
Yes. Only women train in the women's class, and it is taught by a female black belt. The wider academy classes are mixed and open to you as well.
What do I wear?
Comfortable athletic clothes, hair tied back, no jewelry. Rent a gi at the desk for the trial. If you join, you'll train in a blue or white Alliance or Cobrinha gi with a rash guard underneath.
I've never done anything like this. Will I be paired with someone experienced?
Yes, with someone patient. Nobody spars in their first class unless they ask to.
How much is it?
The same memberships as the rest of the academy: from $257 a month for 8 classes to $327 unlimited. Membership details.
