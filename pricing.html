What every membership includes

One price, the whole academy.

World-champion curriculum

Every level follows the curriculum Cobrinha built and teaches, from the beginners course to the advanced room.

Six days a week

Morning, lunchtime and evening classes Monday to Friday, plus Saturday. Fundamentals, Advanced, No-Gi and Women's all have their own slots.

Gi and No-Gi

Both programs are included. Train in the kimono, train without it, or do both.

Belts and stripes

Promotions are earned on the mat and tracked, so you always know where you stand and what's next.

Changing rooms and showers

Men's and women's changing rooms with showers, and a lounge for family and visitors.

Parking with validation

Free before 8:30 am, after 6 pm and on weekends; $5 for two hours midday.

Competition team

Optional for adults and kids who want to test themselves, coached inside the advanced program.

A room that knows your name

Hundreds of students, one head coach, sixteen years on the same corner of Wilshire.

Before you decide

The free class comes first.

Nobody is asked to choose a plan before they've trained. Adults: any Monday or Wednesday at 8 pm, or Thursday at 6:10 pm. Kids: their age group's trial slot. Gear rental is $20 for adults and $15 for kids, refunded in full when you sign up.
