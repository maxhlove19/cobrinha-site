Membership

Membership.

Adults start with the four-week Beginners course or sign up directly, then pick the plan that matches how often you'll actually train, from eight classes a month to unlimited. Memberships renew monthly and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. The whole academy, every instructor and every program is included, and the desk goes through the plans with you after your first class.

What every membership includes

One price, the whole academy.

World-champion curriculum

Every level follows the curriculum Cobrinha built and teaches, from the beginners course to the advanced room.

Six days a week

Morning, lunchtime and evening classes Monday to Friday, plus Saturday. Fundamentals, Advanced, No-Gi and Women's all have their own slots.

Gi and No-Gi

Both programs are included. Train in the kimono, train without it, or do both.

Belts and stripes

Promotions are earned on the mat and tracked, so you always know where you stand and what's next.

Changing rooms and showers

Men's and women's changing rooms with showers, and a lounge for family and visitors.

Parking with validation

Free before 8:30 am, after 6 pm and on weekends; $5 for two hours midday.

Competition team

Optional for adults and kids who want to test themselves, coached inside the advanced program.

A room that knows your name

Hundreds of students, one head coach, 15 years on the same corner of Wilshire.

Before you decide

The Beginners course comes first.

Nobody is asked to choose a plan before they've trained. Adults start with the four-week Beginners course (Monday and Wednesday 8:00 pm, Thursday 6:10 pm, start any week) or sign up directly. Kids and teens get a free trial class in their age group's slot. Gear rental is $20 for adults and $15 for kids, refunded in full when you sign up.
