Who is Cobrinha?

A legend of the sport. He runs this room.

Rubens "Cobrinha" Charles is a five-time IBJJF World Champion, four-time No-Gi World Champion and three-time ADCC Champion, the only black belt ever to complete the Super Grand Slam, and a member of the IBJJF and ADCC Halls of Fame. He opened this academy on Wilshire in 2011.

  • He teaches the advanced program himself and sets the curriculum for every class, beginners included.
  • Every class is led by a black or brown belt he promoted or approved.
  • Part of the Alliance team, one of the most successful teams in the sport's history.

Adults · new to Jiu-Jitsu

The four-week Beginners course.

Built for people who have never trained. Three classes a week for four weeks: how to fall, how to get up, how to escape the positions you'll really end up in, and your first submissions. A black or brown belt watches every rep. Then you join Fundamentals 1.

One on one

Private lessons. The fastest way to get better.

Sixty minutes with a black belt on exactly what you need: a position that keeps beating you, a competition coming up, a comeback after injury, or a schedule that doesn't fit group classes. New to Jiu-Jitsu? A private is the most comfortable way to start.

  • See the open times and book online, no phone tag
  • Single lessons, packs of 5 or 10, or a small group of up to 4
  • Sessions with Cobrinha himself by request
