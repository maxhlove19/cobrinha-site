Rubens "Cobrinha" Charles

The record.

Twelve world titles in the gi, in no-gi and at ADCC, and one year nobody had ever had before. Every result on this page is listed with the division it was won in.

5×IBJJF World Champion
4×No-Gi World Champion
3×ADCC World Champion
5×IBJJF Pan Champion
1×IBJJF European Champion

2017

The Super Grand Slam.

Five majors exist in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. In 2017, at 37 years old, Cobrinha won all five of them in the same year — something no one had done before him.

Every one of these five golds is confirmed in the IBJJF's own championship database or in the ADCC event record. He beat a different finalist in each.

Every result

Year by year.

Black belt from 2006 onward, at featherweight in the gi and at 66 kg at ADCC. 1st is gold, 2nd silver, 3rd bronze, HOF a Hall of Fame induction.

2022

  • HOFADCC Hall of Fame — tenth inductee of the inaugural class, announced January 2022.

2017

  • 1stIBJJF European Championship — black belt, featherweight (70 kg), gi. Beat Gianni Grippo in the final.
  • 1stIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi. Beat Osvaldo "Queixinho" Moizinho in the final.
  • 1stBrazilian National Championship (Brasileiro) — black belt, featherweight, gi. Beat Rafael Mansur in the final.
  • 1stIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi. Beat Leonardo "Cascão" Saggioro in the final.
  • 1stADCC World Championship, Espoo, Finland — 66 kg. Beat AJ Agazarm in the final.

2015

  • 1stADCC World Championship, São Paulo — 66 kg. Beat Bruno Frazatto 3–0 in the final.

2014

  • HOFIBJJF Hall of Fame — inaugural induction class.

2013

  • 1stADCC World Championship, Beijing — 66 kg. Beat Rafael Mendes on points in the final.

2012

  • 1stIBJJF No-Gi World Championship — black belt, featherweight. Beat Justin Rader in the final.
  • 2ndIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi. Lost the final to Rafael Mendes.

2011

  • 1stIBJJF No-Gi World Championship — black belt, featherweight.
  • 2ndADCC World Championship, Nottingham — 66 kg. Lost the final to Rafael Mendes.
  • 2ndWorld Professional Jiu-Jitsu Cup, Abu Dhabi — black belt, absolute (open weight). Lost the final to Rodolfo Vieira.
  • 3rdIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 3rdWorld Professional Jiu-Jitsu Cup, Abu Dhabi — black belt, 74 kg.

2010

  • 1stIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 2ndIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 2ndBrazilian National Championship (Brasileiro) — black belt, featherweight, gi.

2009

  • 1stIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 1stIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 2ndADCC World Championship, Barcelona — 66 kg. Lost the final to Rafael Mendes.
  • 3rdIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, open class (absolute).

2008

  • 1stIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 1stIBJJF No-Gi World Championship — black belt, lightweight. Won a division above his own.
  • 1stIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 1stIBJJF Pan American No-Gi Championship — black belt, lightweight.

2007

  • 1stIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.
  • 1stIBJJF No-Gi World Championship — black belt, featherweight.
  • 1stIBJJF Pan American Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi.

2006

  • 1stIBJJF World Championship — black belt, featherweight, gi. His first world title as a black belt, one year after the promotion.

2005

  • 1stBrazilian National Championship (Brasileiro) — brown belt, featherweight. Won in the year he took his black belt from Fernando "Tereré" Augusto.
