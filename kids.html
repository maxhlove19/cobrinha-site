A message to parents

Why Jiu-Jitsu, not another sport.

Everyone promises self-discipline, self-control and self-confidence. The honest question is how you give those to a child. Our answer: an environment of positive reinforcement, goals they can measure, respect they practice every class, and coaches who stay by their side.

  • Safest martial art to start. No punching or kicking. Technique lets a smaller child control a bigger one, so the training itself is gentle.
  • Bully-proofing that starts with words. Walk away first. If it can't be avoided, know exactly what to do.
  • Goals they can reach. Stripes every few months, belts by level. A child sees their own progress.
  • Respect that travels. Bow on, bow off, shake hands, listen. Parents tell us it shows up at the dinner table.
  • Coordination and focus. Balance, body awareness and attention that carry into every other sport and into school.
  • Fun. Half of every class is games. They're kids.

Parents ask

Good questions.

What does my child wear to the trial class?
Comfortable clothes they can move in. Gi rental for the trial is $15, refunded in full when they sign up. Long hair tied back, no jewelry, nails trimmed.
Can I watch?
Yes. The lounge looks onto the mat. For the youngest we ask parents to stay for the first few classes.
How much is the kids program?
$257 a month. Family pricing is available when siblings or parents train; ask at the desk after the trial.
My child is shy. Will they be pushed?
They'll be invited, not pushed. Coaches partner shy kids with patient ones and let them join at their pace. Most are on the mat by the second class.
How often should they come?
Twice a week is ideal for the little ones; Warriors and Teens can train up to five days.

Your first class is free

Come train with us this week.

No experience needed. Wear comfortable clothes, rent a gi at the desk, and we'll walk you through everything.
