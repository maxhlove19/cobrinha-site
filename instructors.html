The team

Taught by champions.

Every class at the academy is led by a black or brown belt promoted or approved by Cobrinha. These are the people you'll train with.

Rubens "Cobrinha" Charles

Head coach · 4th-degree black belt

Five-time IBJJF World Champion, four-time No-Gi World Champion, three-time ADCC Champion, and the only black belt ever to complete the Super Grand Slam. IBJJF and ADCC Hall of Fame. Teaches the advanced program and sets the curriculum for every level.

Kennedy Maciel

Black belt · adult program

Black belt under Cobrinha. Started training in 2012 after moving to Los Angeles from São Carlos, Brazil. World Champion at every colored belt from white to brown, 2016 European Champion, 2017 Pan and Abu Dhabi World Pro Champion, 2019 ADCC silver medalist. Teaches the adult classes.

Ursula Valverde

Black belt · women's program & privates

Female black belt who leads the women's classes and free trial, and teaches private lessons through the week. She and Paulo, her husband, teach as a team. Focused on self-defense, technique and building confidence in women who are new to the mat.

M

Makoto

Black belt · instructor

Black belt, active competitor and instructor on the academy team; most recently won a professional super-fight by submission in 20 seconds. Follow him at @makoto_bjj.

E

Eduardo

Brown belt · morning program

Brown belt on the academy team. Runs the early-morning Fundamentals classes for people who train before work.

C

Cleber Sousa

Black belt · instructor & competitor

Black belt on the academy team and an active competitor, with a recent first-place finish in the black belt featherweight division. Full bio to come.

P

Paulo

Black belt · instructor

Black belt on the academy team and Ursula's husband; students on Yelp single him out for going above and beyond for beginners. Belt, bio and photo to come from the academy.
