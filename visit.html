Uniform

What to wear.

  • First class: athletic clothes; rent a gi at the desk ($20 adults, $15 kids, refunded when you sign up).
  • Gi classes: a blue or white Alliance or Cobrinha gi with a rash guard underneath. No T-shirts, no other colors or teams' patches: we keep everyone one team.
  • No-Gi classes: academy ranked rash guard with no other affiliation patches, black shorts with black tights underneath. Same for men and women.
  • Don't wear the same gi for two classes in a row. Changing rooms and showers are available.

Drop-ins

Visiting students with experience are welcome. A drop-in class is $60, gi included, and the $60 is refunded if you sign up with us. Call (323) 931-9953 or email info@cobrinhabjj.com ahead of time to reserve a spot, and sign the waiver online before you arrive.

Mat etiquette

How the room works.

These are the habits that keep a room of two hundred people safe and friendly. New students pick them up in a week.

  • Bow when you step on and off the mat. Shake hands with your teammates when you arrive.
  • Introduce yourself to visitors and newcomers. Treat them the way you wanted to be treated on your first day.
  • Full gi and a properly tied belt before you step on. Gi tops and rash guards stay on in the mat and lobby areas.
  • Shoes off before the mat, shoes on the moment you leave it, even for the bathroom.
  • Fingernails and toenails short. No jewelry.
  • No food, gum or phones on the mat. No photos or video during class.
  • Be on time. If you're late, wait at the edge of the mat until the instructor waves you in.
  • Ask the instructor before leaving the mat.
  • Quiet while the instructor is talking; respectful language always, especially around the kids.
  • Shake or touch hands before and after every roll, and thank your partner.
  • Use only as much force as the technique needs. Nobody gets hurt on purpose here.
  • When you're given a drill, keep drilling until you're told otherwise. Then have fun.
