Adults

Fundamentals 1, 2 and 3.

The core of the academy. After the beginners course you join Fundamentals 1 and work through the positions, escapes, sweeps, passes and submissions that every black belt still drills. Stripes move you to Fundamentals 2 (three stripes) and 3 (four stripes).

  • Fundamentals 1: Mon & Wed 8 pm, Tue & Thu 6:10 pm, Tue 6:30 am
  • Fundamentals 2: Mon & Wed 6:30 am, Mon & Wed 6:10 pm, Fri 6:20 pm
  • Fundamentals 3: Saturday 11:15 am · All Levels: Tue & Thu 6:30 am
  • Everyone trains in a blue or white Alliance or Cobrinha gi with a rash guard underneath; one team, one look

Blue belt and above

Advanced & Competition.

Ninety-minute classes taught by Cobrinha and his black belts: the systems that won five World titles, broken down and drilled, then sparred. Competition preparation runs inside this room for anyone who wants to test themselves, from local opens to the Worlds.

  • Mon & Wed 7:20 pm · Mon, Wed & Fri 12:00 pm
  • No-Gi Advanced: Tue & Thu 12:00 pm
  • Visiting blue belts and up are welcome; call ahead. Drop-in $60 with gi included, refunded if you sign up

All levels

No-Gi.

Submission grappling without the kimono: faster, wrestling-heavy, and the base of modern MMA grappling. Six No-Gi classes a week, all on Tuesday and Thursday. No-Gi Fundamentals is open to everyone; the 7:20 class is for white belts with three stripes and up.

  • Tue & Thu 12:00 pm: No-Gi Advanced (blue belt and up)
  • Tue & Thu 6:10 pm: No-Gi Fundamentals (or Gi Fundamentals 1 in the same slot)
  • Tue & Thu 7:20 pm: No-Gi (white belt three stripes and up)
  • Academy ranked rash guard and black shorts

Women only

Women's program.

A class created for women to get stronger, learn real self-defense and build confidence with people who understand what that takes. Taught by Ursula, a female black belt. No experience needed and all levels train together.

  • Tuesday 8:00 am · Saturday 11:15 am
  • Free trial: Wednesday 6:10 pm is the best slot; Tuesday or Thursday 6:10 pm also work
  • Self-defense focus, supportive room, women's changing room and showers
