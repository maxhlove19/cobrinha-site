Adult beginners

I've never done any martial art. Will I be lost?
No. Beginners start in a four-week course built for people with zero experience, then move into Fundamentals with others at the same level. Nobody spars in their first class unless they ask to.
Am I too old or too out of shape?
We have students who started at 50 and 60. Jiu-Jitsu is technique and leverage, not strength, and the pace of the beginners course is set by the room. Fitness comes from the training, not before it.
When is the free trial?
Any Monday or Wednesday at 8:00 pm, or Thursday at 6:10 pm. Gear rental is $20 and is refunded in full when you sign up.
What do I wear to the first class?
Comfortable athletic clothes, and rent a gi at the desk. If you continue, you'll train in an academy gi (white or blue) with a rash guard underneath.
Is it safe? Will I get hurt?
Jiu-Jitsu has no striking. Beginners drill techniques with a partner under a coach's eye, and every roll ends the moment someone taps. Injuries are far rarer than in most team sports.
How often should I come?
Two or three times a week is the sweet spot for beginners. Once a week works; you'll just move a little slower.
How long until a blue belt?
Typically one and a half to two years of consistent training. Stripes come along the way, roughly every few months, so you always know where you stand.
What if I have an old injury?
Tell the coach before class. Almost everything can be adapted, and the beginners course avoids the positions that stress knees and shoulders until you're ready for them.

Cost & membership

How much does it cost?
Adults: Silver $257 a month for 8 classes, Gold $299 for 12, Platinum $327 unlimited. Kids and teens: $257 a month. Month to month, family pricing when more than one of you trains. Membership details.
Is the trial really free?
Yes. The only charge is gear rental, $20 for adults and $15 for kids, and it is refunded in full when you sign up.
Is there a contract?
Memberships are month to month. Ask the front desk for the current terms and the family options.
What do private lessons cost?
Brown belt: $160 for one 60-minute lesson, $725 for five, $1,350 for ten. Black belt: $220, $955 and $1,800. A small group of four is $80 per person. Lessons with Cobrinha are by request. All privates are scheduled in advance with the instructor; no-shows use the lesson.
Can I pause my membership?
Talk to the front desk; holds are handled case by case for travel, injury and school terms.

Visiting & parking

Where do I park?
The building lot, with validation from the front desk: free before 8:30 am, after 6 pm and on weekends; $5 for two hours midday. Free street parking on Highland Ave.
Where exactly is the academy?
4929 Wilshire Blvd, Suite 104, Los Angeles 90010, in the lobby of the office building on the corner of Wilshire and Highland in Hancock Park.
I already train elsewhere. Can I drop in?
Yes. Call or email ahead to reserve a spot, sign the waiver online, bring a white or blue gi, and read the visitor guidelines. Cobrinha's advanced class is blue belt and up.
Is the academy open on Sundays?
Sunday is a members-only open mat at 10 am, all levels. No classes; the front desk opens for members.
Are there showers?
Yes, in both the men's and women's changing rooms.

Kids & teens

Can my child start at 3?
Yes. Baby Eagles is for ages 3–4, Little Eagles 5–6, Eagle Warriors 7–12, Teens 13–16.
When is the kids' free trial?
Ages 3–4: Tuesday or Thursday 3:30 pm (30 minutes). Ages 5–6: Monday or Wednesday 3:30 pm (30 minutes). Ages 7–12: any Monday to Thursday at 5:00 pm. Teens 13–16: Tuesday or Thursday 5:00 pm. Gear rental is $15, refunded in full when they sign up.
What does my child wear to the trial class?
Comfortable clothes they can move in; rent a gi at the desk. Long hair tied back, no jewelry, nails trimmed.
Can I watch?
Yes. The lounge looks onto the mat. For the youngest we ask parents to stay for the first few classes.
How much is the kids program?
$257 a month, month to month. Family pricing applies when siblings or parents train; ask at the desk after the trial.
My child is shy. Will they be pushed?
They'll be invited, not pushed. Coaches partner shy kids with patient ones and let them join at their pace. Most are on the mat by the second class.
Does my child have to compete?
No. Competition is optional. For the kids who ask, there is a competition team coached the way Cobrinha coached world champions.
How often should they come?
Twice a week is ideal for the little ones; Warriors and Teens can train up to five days.

Women's program

Is the women's class really women only?
Yes. It is taught by Ursula Valverde, a female black belt, and only women train in it. Tuesday 8 am and Saturday 11:15 am. Free trial: Wednesday 6:10 pm is the best slot; Tuesday or Thursday 6:10 pm also work.
Can women train in the regular classes too?
Of course. Many do both. The women's class is an option, not a limit.

Programs

What's the difference between Gi and No-Gi?
Gi is the traditional kimono, with grips on the collar and sleeves; No-Gi is a rash guard and shorts, faster and closer to wrestling. Both are taught here. Beginners usually start in the gi.
What are the levels?
Beginners course, then Fundamentals 1 (all levels), Fundamentals 2 (three stripes), Fundamentals 3 (four stripes), then Advanced (blue belt and up), which Cobrinha teaches.
Does Cobrinha teach?
Yes. He teaches the advanced program every week and sets the curriculum for every level; every class is led by a black or brown belt he promoted or approved.
Do you offer online training?
Yes, through Cobrinha Online, for training at home or while traveling.
